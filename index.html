Interpreting this estimate

This model was developed in 307 patients with pathologically confirmed lung adenosquamous carcinoma. Risk groups use the cohort median score (171.1751, rounded); patients at or below the original median are classified as low risk. Estimates require external validation and should support, rather than determine, clinical decisions. Surgical status reflects an observed association and does not estimate an individual treatment benefit.

Model information and calculation method

Five predictors are included: age, pulmonary metastasis, liver metastasis, surgical resection, and serum albumin. Time is measured from the study's original OS origin; 1-, 3-, and 5-year predictions use the fitted Cox survival function.

For an adverse-factor indicator vector x, S(t | x) = exp[log(Sreference(t)) × exp(Σβixi)]. The zero-point reference is age <65 years, no pulmonary or liver metastasis, surgical resection, and albumin ≥40 g/L. Point totals retain the original score formula; survival probabilities use the full-precision coefficients and baseline survival.

All calculations run locally in your browser. The calculator contains aggregate model parameters only. It does not collect patient identifiers, store inputs, or send patient data to a server. The hospital emblem is sourced from the hospital's official website.
